Phase 16.7.8 step 7v — lxml gate: the push-parser blocker is fixed; the
remaining lxml failures are PRE-EXISTING and independent of this work.

WHY THIS RECEIPT EXISTS
-----------------------
The step-7v option flip makes lxml's default parse-option set
(NOENT | NOCDATA | NONET | COMPACT | BIG_LINES) eligible for the persistent
push driver. Before the flip every lxml parse stayed on the O(N^2) replay
engine and lxml's 2.2 GB test_very_large_sourceline_iterparse hung for more
than 240 s. After the flip the suite runs past that test (20.5 s in the debug
build the court uses; ~2.4 GB/s in release) and then reaches two PRE-EXISTING
candidate defects that the hang had been masking.

THE TWO PRE-EXISTING DEFECTS (both reproduce on the pre-change commit)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. lxml.etree.Schematron doctest / xmlSchematronNewValidCtxt clones a
   CompiledExpr and segfaults (src/xml/xpath/ast.rs:277). This is the test
   that aborts `python3 test.py` (rc=139), so the suite cannot complete.

2. lxml.isoschematron: 21 of 25 tests fail. `iso_dsdl_include.xsl`
   (a 4 KB XSLT 1.0 stylesheet) produces `<?DSDL_INCLUDE_START?>
   <?DSDL_INCLUDE_END?>` instead of the copied source schema — i.e. the
   generic `node()` copy template never runs. Reproduced OUTSIDE lxml with a
   direct libxslt probe, so it is not an lxml-extension artefact.
